Templates and matching: lag-maximised cosine similarity of templates, merge suggestions, and
matching pursuit of a recording against templates (on the runtime's device).

Templates are `(units, rows, samples)`; row `r` of unit `u` is recording channel
`channels[u, r]` (default: rows are channels `0..rows`).

| | |
|---|---|
| [`matching_pursuit`](#matching_pursuit) | Spikes of `data` `(channels, samples)` found by subtracting the best-fitting template pass after pass. |
| [`suggest_merges`](#suggest_merges) | `(i, j, similarity)` of the unit pairs at least `threshold` alike. |
| [`template_similarity`](#template_similarity) | `(units, units)` cosine similarity, each pair at its best lag (up to `max_lag_samples`). |
